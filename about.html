What this app does

Set up a minimal, runnable web app in this repository for Simple Notes App. Acceptance criteria: - The repo includes a homepage that loads in a browser for Simple Notes App. - A newcomer can start the app with the documented command(s). - The homepage identifies the product as Simple Notes App. - The scaffold is committed and ready for follow-up tickets.

How it works

  • Capture a note with a title and optional details.
  • Browse every saved note in the list on the home page.
  • Notes are stored in your browser with localStorage, so nothing leaves your device.
