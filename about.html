What this app does

Notes App is a lightweight notes application with note creation, a list view, and this About screen that explains the app: capture notes, browse them, and understand why it exists.

How it works

  • Capture a note with a title and optional details.
  • Browse every saved note in the list on the home page.
  • Notes are stored in your browser with localStorage, so nothing leaves your device.
